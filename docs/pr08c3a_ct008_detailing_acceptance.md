# PR-08C.3a CT-008 detailing acceptance (clear-spacing and transverse links)

This matrix freezes the first CT-008 slice from retained main mechanics. Sector
remains `0.91`. Rejected PRs are defect evidence only, never implementation
sources. The slice covers two of the CT-008 subfamilies: pairwise clear
spacing and transverse link detailing. The longitudinal minimum-reinforcement
subfamily and the Formula (6.31) shear-torsion minimum-reinforcement screen
are the next slice (PR-08C.3b) and remain named exclusions here; both slices
extend one `ct-008` registry in which every present subfamily is independently
mandatory.

Neither member depends on the other; an absent flag publishes no member and a supplied member for an absent flag fails. The retained kernel's own `NOT APPLICABLE` (no active shear or torsion rows) and `INVALID`/`NOT ASSESSED` states are legitimate finite evidence states, not failures. |
