# PR-08D.5 CT-006 chord and CT-002 join acceptance

This matrix freezes the last unpublished PR-08D implementation slice. Sector
remains `0.91`. It extends the accepted CT-006 directional shear family with
the retained longitudinal-force and chord payloads and joins only the accepted
CT-002 replay contract; it does not create another plastic or shear solver.

| Family or invariant | Frozen acceptance |
| --- | --- |
| Scope and identity | The family remains exactly two ordered active directions, `vx` then `vy`, with physical axes `y` and `x`. Aggregate `biaxial` must be exact Boolean `true`, and the retained note must state that both directions are calculated independently and that no generic cross-direction interaction is calculated. The existing directional shear utilisation and verdict remain the CT-006 final; chord outcomes do not invent a cross-direction or global verdict. |
| CT-002 join | When links are enabled, original `mode`, `check_util`, requested sweep start/end/increment, normalised solver start/end/increment, exact angle cardinality and closed state are mandatory identities. Plastic output presence must agree with `Plastic`/`Both` mode. Chord capacity is available only for a requested, closed, checked sweep whose retained `util` is non-null. Available evidence is reconstructed through the accepted CT-002 solver replay, including exact points, radial result and independently derived `min/max Mx/My`; cached extrema cannot select the chord. Elastic, partial-sweep and unchecked-plastic branches publish explicit unavailable state and no chord candidate. |
| Original-input inventory | The complete link branch pins the selected link-steel identity, link geometry and strut band, lower aggregate size, torsion activation/method/wall thickness/detailing flag/partial factor/demand/subdivision flag and every ordered subrectangle coordinate, plus combined activation and the independent M-V option. Missing fields, incompatible retained types, non-finite values, unknown 2004-family methods, duplicate material identities and unaligned material laws fail closed. Inputs that are genuinely outside the link/chord branch remain inert. |
| Longitudinal shear force | `delta_Ftd = 0.5 VEd cot(theta)` is reconstructed from the original directional demand and independently selected shared angle. The value, duplicate retained force, symbol `delta_Ftd`, and clause text `6.2.3(7), Formula (6.18)` are exact ordered candidate fields. The engineering source is DS/EN 1992-1-1:2004/AC, clause 6.2.3(7), Formula (6.18). |
| Chord mechanics | For each retained candidate, reconstruct applied face moment, conditional `MRd`, shear and torsion longitudinal forces, lever arm, shear/torsion moment increments, total moment, utilisation, demand/resistance verdict, cap state, role, axis, face and selector mode using accepted low-level kernels. Shear force is capped by the retained 2005 rule; conditional-solve failure uses the existing tension-face pure-axis/sweep-extremum fallback only where the accepted mechanics do so. Torsion longitudinal force carries DS/EN 1992-1-1:2004/AC clause 6.3.2(3), Formula (6.28); selector, conditional-capacity and chord-composition rules are uncited Sector project mechanics. |
| Candidate ordering and selectors | `chord_candidates` is exact `lchecks + ochecks`: shear-axis faces first in solver order, then off-axis faces in solver order. `chord` is the first highest-utilisation shear-axis candidate and `chord_off` is the first highest-utilisation off-axis candidate. Missing, duplicate, reordered, unknown or coherently resealed candidate fields and selectors fail. `off_util` is reconstructed from original signed off-axis action and independently replayed signed sweep extrema, never from cached candidate extrema. |
| Torsion and incomplete branches | No torsion produces one shear-axis chord candidate and no off-axis candidate. Live single-tube torsion requires both shear-axis and both off-axis faces when their conditional solves are defensible. `off_not_evaluated` is exactly `None`, `subdivided`, or `not_solved`: subdivision discloses per-subtube torsion steel; `not_solved` discloses an incomplete conditional face. No omitted face is replaced with fabricated resistance, utilisation or PASS. CT-007 mechanics and verdicts remain separate even though the accepted torsion kernel supplies the shared selector and longitudinal force. |
| Shared selector | Replay the retained 1,501-point minimax over every live accepted operand: link yielding and crushing; live torsion subtubes; shared transverse and crushing interactions; every defensible shear-axis and off-axis chord; and the retained combined DK expression when active. Invalid or unloaded companions do not constrain the angle. Capacity-only state keeps the accepted resistance selector. Candidate-selected cotangent, branch, chord, utilisation or verdict is never trusted. |
| Failure-first behaviour | Activity, exact original-input identities, concrete/link applicability and base finite state are established before untrusted plastic numerical evidence is replayed. A base shear failure publishes only failure evidence; malformed failure-only shear or plastic numerical payloads cannot mask it. Promotion to finite traverses the complete CT-002, shear, shared-selector and chord reconstruction. Every finite advertised field reaches the directional final. |
| Provenance and exclusions | The implemented 2004+A1/AC and DK NA:2024 shear variants remain supported; 2023 material or shear sources remain outside CT-006. This slice changes no solver formula, UI, report, manual, persistence, package, schema, workflow or application version. CT-007/008 publication, activation wiring, PR-09 through PR-14, v0.93 candidates and all rejected heads remain excluded. |

Focused evidence must cover base and DK shear, available and unavailable plastic
branches, live torsion, subdivision, incomplete off-axis disclosure, exact
candidate inventories, coherent selector/chord tampering, failed-state masking,
registry closure and exact source identity. Publication activation and the
